# AG-2 — Vendor card contact polish

**Type:** Feature · **Agentic**

Vendor cards show a raw email address and an inconsistently formatted phone number.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Phone numbers on vendor cards use the shared `formatPhoneNumber` helper
- [ ] Email addresses render as `mailto:` links
- [ ] A vendor with no phone or no email renders cleanly with no stray punctuation or
      console errors
- [ ] No change to any other card type
